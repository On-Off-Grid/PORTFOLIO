About me

About me

I'm Souhail Ouichou, based in Morocco. Before software, I worked in construction and retail operations, where plans met delays, limited resources and real people.

That background still shapes how I build: inspect the system, find the constraint, communicate clearly, and make the next useful thing work. My technical base is modest but real: C, C++, Linux, processes, threads, sockets and Docker.

More recently, I've been exploring LLM agents, orchestration and security automation — sometimes writing the implementation myself, sometimes directing agents through specifications, tests and review.

I left 1337 without completing the cursus. The experience still gave me difficult projects and a stronger preference for honest work over borrowed appearances. I'm now consolidating those foundations, building independently, and looking for an internship where curiosity, accountability and visible learning matter.
